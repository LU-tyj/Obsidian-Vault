在本章，我们介绍所有玩家的大量的行为行为要如何设计。

The patterns in this chapter help to quickly define and refine a large quantity of maintainable behavior. [Type Objects](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/type-object.html) create categories of behavior without the rigidity of defining an actual class. A [Subclass Sandbox](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/subclass-sandbox.html) gives you a safe set of primitives you can use to define a variety of behaviors. The most advanced option is [Bytecode](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/bytecode.html), which moves behavior out of code entirely and into data.
